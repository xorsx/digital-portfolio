01 / The Problem

A nonprofit Google could barely read.

Utica Creative Reuse is a 501(c)3 arts and crafts thrift store in Central New York. The site had crawl errors, unindexed pages and no analytics when I started.

02 / What I Did

What I did.

Skill Before After
Technical SEO Critical crawl errors, pages not indexed Errors resolved, full indexing confirmed
On-page SEO Titles and metas missing or duplicated Unique, keyword-aligned titles and metas
Site architecture No internal link structure Link architecture connecting key pages
Measurement No GA4, no GSC Full tracking from day one
