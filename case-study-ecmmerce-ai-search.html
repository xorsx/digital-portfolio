01 / The Challenge

A premium brand with room to grow in organic.

This client is a direct-to-consumer retail brand in the premium category. My audit set three goals: optimize metadata, expand product and collection content, and bring a dormant blog back into search.

I led the research, analysis and implementation.

02 / Technical SEO

Fix what was holding pages back.

I started with redirects, duplicate and incorrect metadata, URL corrections and collection naming. Then I rewrote titles, descriptions and on-page copy for the highest-priority product and collection pages.

03 / AI Search and GEO

Testing how AI understood the brand.

I ran a query fan-out analysis across the site using the ChatGPT API in Screaming Frog. Giving the model limited context made the output a direct test of how clearly each name identified the brand and product.

Page How the model read it
Brand homepage A company in an unrelated industry
Brand story page A movement in a different sector
Core product An accessory for an entirely different use
Signature product An item from a competing brand
04 / Entity Strategy

Solving an identity problem.

Search data confirmed it: generic terms using part of the brand's name far outpaced searches for the brand itself.

I used that to rank the next pages by ranking upside, commercial value and AI risk, and wrote copy that pairs distinctive product names with plain descriptive terms in titles, H1s and body text.

05 / Results

Year over year, over five months.

Most months beat the prior year, and growth started from a small baseline.

Organic metric Change
Revenue More than doubled
Orders More than tripled
Conversion rate About doubled
Non-brand search clicks Up by more than half
Non-brand queries on page one More than doubled
06 / What Came Next

Turning rankings into revenue.

One core category moved from the third page of results to the upper half of page one on its main terms. I also found that a large share of page-one rankings were image packs, which rank well and earn few clicks. That set the next priority: image optimization and product schema.

These results reflect the technical fixes and first round of page updates, with later updates still to come.
