01 / The Challenge

Organic leads were falling. The question was why.

This client is a multi-location organization in the education sector, where organic search drives a large share of enrollment leads. Organic leads had dropped by a double-digit percentage year over year.

I led the analysis end to end: define what actually happened, separate real losses from reporting artifacts, and turn the answer into a prioritized plan.

02 / Data Analysis

Follow the lead, not the channel.

One analytics report could only confirm that organic was down. I combined five sources: GA4, page and query level Search Console data, Google Business Profile reports, the client's own call and form tracking, and indexing exports.

Total leads across all channels were essentially flat. Gains in other channels offset most of the organic decline, so the problem was far narrower than the headline number suggested.

03 / Local SEO

Finding the channel nobody was counting.

Calls placed directly from Google Business Profiles approached the volume of every lead the websites tracked combined, and none of them reached website analytics. Prospective students were converting on the results page without ever visiting the site.

04 / Search Behavior and AI

Ranking first stopped meaning the same thing.

Branded clicks fell sharply even though each location still ranked first, and click-through at the top position declined. The likely destinations were call buttons, map results and AI answers.

Search Console does not separate clicks lost to AI answers, so I reported that as a likely factor and did not put a number on it. The new AI Assistant channel was small, but it converted better than organic search.

05 / Technical Diagnosis

Ruling out the obvious suspect.

Indexed pages dropped sharply during the same period, which made indexing the first place anyone would look. The excluded URLs were low-value system pages and outdated documents, and no core pages were affected. Calls kept growing through those weeks.

The real losses were more specific: a defined set of core pages losing clicks and leads across every channel, and a site change that moved some conversions to an outside tool nobody was measuring.

06 / Strategy

Turning the diagnosis into a plan.

Each recommendation tied directly to a finding, in priority order.

Priority Skill applied
Measure and optimize local profiles Local SEO and call tracking
Bring untracked conversions into reporting Analytics and measurement
Rebuild core pages around direct answers On-page SEO and AI search optimization
Strengthen branded search results Structured data and SERP strategy
Build AI search visibility GEO and AEO

The result.

The headline number suggested SEO was failing. The analysis showed a shift in how people convert, a measurement gap and a short list of real problems to fix. Implementation is underway, and I will add outcome data once a full comparison period is available.
