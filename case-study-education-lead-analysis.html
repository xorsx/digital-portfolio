01 / The Problem

The report said organic was losing leads.

Organic search drives about half of this client's enrollment leads, and those leads fell 10% year over year. Total leads across every channel moved less than 1%.

02 / What I Found

Six findings the top-line report missed.

Skill Finding What I did
Attribution Gains in other channels offset 90% of the organic loss Reframed a traffic crisis as a channel shift
Local SEO Business Profile calls equaled 90% of all website-tracked leads, with none in analytics Built the plan to measure and optimize them
SERP analysis Branded clicks fell 30%+ while still ranking #1 Prioritized branded results and profile conversion
Technical SEO Thousands of URLs dropped from the index, zero core pages among them Ruled out indexing as the cause
Measurement Thousands of monthly clicks went to a sign-up tool nobody tracked, as forms fell 21% on that site Specified how to count them as leads
AI search AI Assistant leads converted 1.4x better than organic Prioritized answer-first rewrites of core pages
03 / Method

Five data sources, one standard.

GA4, Search Console, Business Profile reports, client call tracking and indexing exports. I used clicks as the main search measure because Google's September 2025 change made impression and position comparisons unreliable.

04 / What's Next

The plan is in motion.

Implementation started in November 2026 with redirects and profile corrections. Next up: profile call tracking, conversion reporting and answer-first rebuilds of the pages losing leads. I will add outcomes once a full comparison period is available.
