What I Do

Technical SEO Audit

I crawl the whole site, find what's keeping pages from being indexed and ranked, and put the fixes in order of what matters most. I've done this for enterprise sites and early-stage startups.

  • Crawl error analysis
  • Indexing audit & fix plan
  • Site architecture review
  • Internal linking analysis
  • Canonicalization & redirect audit
  • Core Web Vitals assessment
  • Structured data / schema review
  • Prioritized recommendations

On-Site Optimization

Keyword mapping, titles and metadata, internal linking and content gaps. I started as a writer, so I'll happily write when it's needed. Mostly I make sure the whole site lines up with how people search.

  • Keyword research & mapping
  • Title tag & meta data optimization
  • Internal linking strategy
  • Content gap & opportunity analysis
  • EEAT signal assessment
  • On-page content recommendations
  • URL structure review

Reporting & Analytics Setup

I set up GA4 and Search Console properly, repair what's broken, and build dashboards people actually open. Every report ties search back to revenue or leads.

  • GA4 setup & configuration
  • GSC setup & property verification
  • Conversion tracking
  • Looker Studio dashboards
  • Keyword & ranking tracking
  • Monthly performance reporting
  • Stakeholder-ready reporting templates

Organic Growth Strategy

This is the part I love most. I dig through performance data, find the biggest opportunities and build a roadmap a team can follow. It's what I do every day across my client accounts.

  • Competitive landscape analysis
  • Organic growth roadmap
  • Prioritization framework
  • Monthly strategy calls
  • Ongoing performance analysis
  • GEO / AI search optimization
